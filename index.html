Hand mask refinement with MediaPipe + GrabCut

MediaPipe gives a rough hand prior from landmarks. OpenCV GrabCut refines that prior into a mask. The result is drawn as a semi-transparent red overlay on the webcam feed.

Tip: GrabCut is colour-sensitive. It works best with a hand against a visually different background and may struggle with skin-coloured objects, motion blur, or very small hands.
